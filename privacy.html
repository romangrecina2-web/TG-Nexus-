Privacy Policy

This Policy describes information TG Nexus processes when you use its Telegram bot and Mini App. It reflects the current project implementation and may be updated as storage and deletion features change. Read the Terms of Service as well.

1. Information processed

  • Telegram account information: your Telegram user identifier and information Telegram includes with messages or Mini App authentication.
  • Content: prompts, bot messages, chat history, and text or image attachments you choose to send to an AI model.
  • Settings: selected provider, API endpoint, model, preferences and configuration needed to run the service.
  • Credentials: API keys you choose to connect. Depending on the feature and Telegram client, a key may be kept in Telegram SecureStorage and/or synchronized to encrypted server-side storage.
  • Consent record: your Telegram user identifier, the Terms version accepted and the time of acceptance.

2. Purposes

Information is processed to authenticate your Telegram session, operate the bot and Mini App, keep and synchronize chats and settings, send requests to your selected AI provider, protect the service from abuse, and troubleshoot technical problems.

3. Where information goes

  • Telegram: handles bot messages and Mini App authentication as part of the Telegram platform.
  • TG Nexus backend: stores synchronized settings, chat history and supported credentials in an encrypted server-side store using AES-256-GCM encryption.
  • Your device: the Mini App may keep application data in browser storage. API keys may use Telegram SecureStorage when available.
  • AI providers: prompts and attachments are sent to the provider and model you select. In multi-agent mode, task context may also be sent to additional configured providers.
  • Hosting infrastructure: hosting providers may process technical connection and operational data.

4. Third-party provider policies

External AI providers apply their own privacy, logging, retention and content-handling rules. TG Nexus does not control how a selected provider handles a request after receiving it. Review the provider's policy before sending information, and avoid submitting confidential or sensitive personal data.

5. Storage and deletion

Information is kept in local app storage or server-side storage for as long as it is needed to provide chat history, synchronization and service operation. Encrypted storage may include a backup snapshot.

Important current limitation: removing a chat from the Mini App does not currently guarantee that every synchronized server-side copy or backup is immediately removed. The current version does not provide a complete self-service account-data deletion control. For access, correction or deletion requests, contact the Nexus Telegram Channel. Requests may require manual review and may be limited by current technical capabilities and applicable requirements.

6. Security

TG Nexus uses HTTPS for supported network connections, Telegram authentication checks, encrypted server-side storage and Telegram SecureStorage where available. These safeguards reduce risk but cannot guarantee absolute security. Protect your Telegram account and API credentials.

7. Logs and sharing

The backend is designed not to write message contents, Telegram user identifiers, API keys or webhook secrets into its application error logs. This does not prevent Telegram, hosting providers or AI providers from processing technical information under their own policies. TG Nexus does not sell personal information.

8. Your choices and requests

You choose which provider and model to connect and what information to submit. You can stop using the service at any time. For questions or requests concerning information handled by TG Nexus, contact the channel below. Do not post API keys or other credentials publicly.

9. Policy updates

This Policy may change when the service, its providers or its data-handling features change. The current version is published on this page.
